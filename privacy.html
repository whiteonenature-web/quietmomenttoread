This online Bible reading and conversation programme is organised by:

Where relevant, this programme may involve joint processing arrangements with Meta Platforms Ireland Limited in connection with the Facebook Page and advertising tools used to promote this programme. For information about Meta's own processing of your data, please see Meta's Privacy Policy.

2.Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we process personal data when you visit our programme website, register for a session, receive practical session information, or voluntarily contact us. The programme is offered online through Zoom. We aim to process personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and applicable Belgian data protection law, including the Act of 30 July 2018 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data.

6.Zoom

Sessions are conducted using Zoom. When you join a session, Zoom may process certain technical information (such as your display name and connection data) in accordance with Zoom's own privacy documentation. We do not intentionally collect additional personal information through the programme beyond what is necessary for the meeting. Sessions are not recorded.

See Zoom's current privacy statement: zoom.com/en/trust/privacy/privacy-statement .

7.International Transfers

Personal data may be accessed by organisers located in the Republic of Korea. Such transfers are made on the basis of the European Commission's adequacy decision for the Republic of Korea (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2022/254 of 17 December 2021), which permits transfers without additional safeguards, subject to the conditions of that decision.

Personal data processed via Zoom may be transferred to the United States. Where the relevant Zoom entity is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, that certification serves as the transfer mechanism; otherwise, Standard Contractual Clauses apply.

8.Who May Receive the Data

Organisers responsible for administering the programme; service providers necessary to operate registration, email and Zoom; and authorities where disclosure is required by law. We do not sell participant data or share it with unrelated organisations for their own marketing purposes.

9.Retention

Registration data is retained for 90 days after the relevant session and then deleted or anonymised, unless a longer period is required by law or for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. Marketing consent data is retained until consent is withdrawn or, absent engagement, for 1 year, subject to periodic re-confirmation.

10.Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures, proportionate to the low-risk nature of this processing, to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure, including access restriction and secure account practices.

11.Your Rights

Subject to the conditions of the GDPR, you may request access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, object to processing, request data portability, withdraw consent at any time, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Requests concerning your personal data may be sent to whiteonenature@gmail.com.

12.Complaints — Belgium

If you are located in Belgium, you may contact the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données):

www.gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be

You may also contact the supervisory authority of another EU/EEA country where applicable.

13.Cookies

This website does not use non-essential analytics, advertising pixels or behavioural tracking technologies. If this changes in the future, this section will be updated and a consent banner will be added in accordance with Article 129 of the Belgian Act of 13 June 2005 on Electronic Communications, before any such technology is activated.

14.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our processing activities, service providers, or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page, with the date of the most recent update shown at the top.
